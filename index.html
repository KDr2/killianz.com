Shine, the fragments!

Killian Q. Zhuo

Foreword

If life were to be a book, it would not be a linear narrative bound by chronology, but a palimpsest of fragments — some polished into Euclidean precision, others preserved as raw whispers of a moment. To hold the pen is to declare: even the most ephemeral thought deserves a chance at a trace.
