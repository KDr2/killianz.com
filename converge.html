4 Converge, the uncertain!

The world gives us fragments, not answers. We infer what we can from the scraps—never certain, always provisional—and act anyway. To converge upon anything real in such a fog is not a matter of perfect information, but of better priors and the courage to revise them.

This chapter documents that process. Thoughts on how to think clearly when the map is incomplete, how to decide well when the stakes are unknown, and how to keep moving forward without the luxury of certainty.
