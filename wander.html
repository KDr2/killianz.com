3 Wander, the unbound.

To wander is not to be lost. It is to refuse the shortest path in favor of the interesting one—to follow a thought because it pulls, not because it leads somewhere useful. The unbound mind does not optimize; it explores.

This chapter is that exploration. Notes from the margins of life, from books and streets and conversations, from anywhere the mind goes when no one is telling it where to stop.
